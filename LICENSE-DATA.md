# Data license

The files under `data/` and `results/`, the template library under `generator/v2/templates/`, and every
string regenerated by `generator/generate.py`, `generator/v2/generate_v2.py` and `policy/build_policy.py` are **fully synthetic**: names, identifiers, dates, phone numbers
(555-01xx), email addresses (@example.test), facilities and message texts are fabricated
for benchmark purposes only. No real person, organization, or patient record is described.
